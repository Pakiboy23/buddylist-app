## LinkedIn founder post

**Use with:** `x-linkedin-launch-1200x675.png`

Today, Apple approved H.I.M. version 2.2, and it is now live on the App Store.

The release is centered on one product decision: the Buddy List should be the home of the app, not a secondary directory.

Your profile now sits at the top of the list. Suggested Buddies use mutual buddies and shared rooms to provide context. Buddy Circles let people organize friendships privately. Knock offers a low-pressure way to say “you around?”

The broader lesson for me was that product mechanics carry values.

A swipe asks for a verdict. A proximity grid prioritizes whoever is nearest. A buddy list assumes a relationship can persist.

H.I.M. is built for gay friendship, conversation, and community. Version 2.2 makes that intent much clearer in the product itself.

There is more to improve, as always. Today I am grateful to put this one into people’s hands.

Download H.I.M. 2.2: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/h-i-m-friends-not-dates/id6761863631
