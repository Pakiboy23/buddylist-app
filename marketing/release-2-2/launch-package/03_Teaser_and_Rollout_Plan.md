# Seven-day launch and feature-reveal plan

## Strategy in one sentence

Launch the release as one product story, then spend a week revealing the individual behaviors that make that story true.

Version 2.2 is already live, so the “teasers” below are feature reveals, not coming-soon promises.

## Day 0: Tuesday, August 4

### Gate: before public posting

- Complete the production smoke test in `05_Launch_Checklist_and_Metrics.md`.
- Capture starting App Store and website metrics.
- Confirm the direct App Store URL resolves to version 2.2.
- Update the persistent bio link with the campaign UTM.

### Launch sequence

| Timing in ET | Publish | Asset | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| As soon as the smoke test passes | Substack article | `substack-cover-1600x900.png` | Establish the product idea and founder voice |
| 15 minutes later | Instagram carousel + Threads | Six numbered Instagram slides | Explain the release in a saveable format |
| 30 minutes later | X thread | `x-linkedin-launch-1200x675.png` on post 1 | Drive discussion and direct App Store clicks |
| 45 minutes later | LinkedIn founder post | `x-linkedin-launch-1200x675.png` | Share the product lesson and founder milestone |
| Immediately after feed posts | Instagram Stories | `story-launch-1080x1920.png` | Drive taps with a direct App Store link sticker |
| Two to four hours later | 30-second Reel or TikTok | Real product screen recording | Show that the features are real and usable |

### Day 0 operating notes

- Pin the launch post on Instagram, Threads, and X.
- Reply to every substantive comment on launch day.
- Ask follow-up questions rather than answering every comment with “thanks.”
- Save bugs, support issues, and feature requests in separate lists.
- Do not debate whether the app is a dating app. Answer once: “H.I.M. is designed for friendship, conversation, and community.”

## Day 6: Monday, August 10

### Build-in-public note

Share one honest observation from the first five days. Use a real number only if it has been verified and has context.

Suggested structure:

1. “H.I.M. 2.2 has been live for six days.”
2. One thing people understood immediately.
3. One point of confusion or friction.
4. One fix or experiment you are making next.
5. A thank-you and App Store link.

Avoid vanity-only screenshots. A small, interpretable signal is more credible than a wall of analytics.

## Optional second-week extensions

- A 60-second founder video on why product mechanics carry values.
- A member prompt about screennames and online identity, using only volunteered responses.
- A short demo comparing a cold introduction with mutual context.
- A “three small things in 2.2” post covering away-message replies, Privacy Controls, and room seen counts.
- A direct feedback session or office hour in a room, with the time clearly announced.

## Channel rules

| Channel | Use it for | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Substack | Product philosophy, founder context, durable launch narrative | Release-note laundry lists |
| Instagram | Visual explanation, feature reveals, Stories, demos | Dense text in captions without a clear hook |
| Threads | Conversational versions of launch and feature posts | Posting the same link repeatedly without discussion |
| X | Product thread, founder notes, fast issue updates | Seven separate launch tweets that compete with the thread |
| LinkedIn | Product decision and founder milestone | Consumer-style feature dumping |
| TikTok / Reels | Real interface, founder voice, one behavior per video | Mock screens that could be mistaken for the product |
| Reddit | Contextual participation in relevant conversations | Launch blasts or copied promotional posts |

## Engagement response bank

### “Is this a dating app?”

No. H.I.M. is designed for gay friendship, conversation, and community. The product is built around rooms, away messages, and a persistent Buddy List rather than swiping or proximity.

### “Why retro?”

The visual language is warm and familiar, but the goal is not nostalgia for its own sake. It is about bringing conversation and personality back to the center.

### “Is Android coming?”

H.I.M. is on iPhone today. I am not announcing an Android timeline yet, but I am paying attention to the demand.

### Bug report

Thank you. Please send the screen you were on, what you tapped, what happened, and a screenshot if possible. I am tracking launch issues closely.

### Feature request

That is useful. What would you be trying to do when you reached for that feature?
